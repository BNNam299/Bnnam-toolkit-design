## 1. Picking the apps
5–8 apps across the spectrum:
- **Leaders**: top results for the 2–3 main keywords and the category chart in the target country.
- **Newcomers gaining fast**: launched in the last 12–24 months and already in the top results — proof the group is not sealed.
- **Growing but complained about**: more valuable than polished leaders; their complaints are the opportunity list for step 3.
- **1–2 indirect alternatives**: a different kind of product that does the same job (a built-in OS feature, a web tool, doing nothing).

Use the same country as the target tier — the way apps monetize differs by country.

**Try to disprove early** (lesson from the Pose project): every "chưa ai làm X" in the idea gets a direct store search on day one,
about 30 minutes, before deeper work. One app already doing X well can end the direction cheaply — say so in §2.

## 2. What to measure and how to frame size
Per app (only what the scan's question needs):

| Measure | Question it answers | Where from |
|---|---|---|
| Downloads / month now and 3 / 12 months ago | how big, which direction | AppStoreSpy / Sensor Tower / `raw/`; else rating-count estimate |
| Revenue / month and revenue ÷ downloads | do users pay | same |
| Launch date, days since last update | still invested in? newcomers breaking in? | store page |
| Rating and rating count growth | traction now, not history | store page / tool |
| Price of the main plans | rough price anchor | store page (In-App Purchases) |

Derived numbers for the table: change over 3 months (▲ ▼ %), revenue per download, share of the top 3 in the group's downloads, how
many apps launched in the last 12 months reached the top results.

**Is the group alive?** Put the group's 3–6 apps on the same 12-month download chart: ≥ 3 rising → a living market · one rising,
the rest flat → find out what that one does right before concluding · the whole group flat or falling → warning: the gap we see may
be a place with no money. Write which case it is in the conclusion.

Frame size like this: "5 app dẫn đầu có tổng ~1,2 triệu lượt tải/tháng ở Mỹ (Sensor Tower, 10/2026, ước tính); 2 app ra mắt năm 2025
đã vào top 10" — evidence first, one sentence. No "$X billion by 2030".

Without tool data, estimate downloads from rating counts (~0,5–2% of downloaders leave a rating), labelled "ước tính từ số đánh giá",
and use it only to compare apps with each other.

## 3. Keywords
Do not research broad words ("cleaner", "drawing", "productivity") — they hide intent. Build keywords in five directions:

| Direction | Example (storage cleaning) |
|---|---|
| Problem | storage full, duplicate photos |
| Desired result | free up storage, organize photos |
| Feature | duplicate photo cleaner |
| Audience / context | iPhone storage cleaner |
| Workaround | delete photos fast |

For the 2–3 main ones record: search volume, difficulty, who ranks top, and the trend in **three time frames** — 90 days (new
movement), 12 months (steady growth and seasonality), 5 years (long-term or short-lived). Analyse per country, never a global blend.
Google Trends values are *relative* interest (0–100), not volume — one signal among several. A high ad price (CPC) means businesses
pay for the word; it does not prove a consumer app will succeed.

## 4. Real demand vs passing trend
| Real demand | Passing trend |
|---|---|
| several related keywords rise together | one keyword spikes |
| many independent discussions | one viral video |
| users already use a workaround | curiosity without repeat use |
| several apps in the group grow together | downloads high but reviews say "tried once" |
| repeated behaviour + willingness to pay | hard to say why anyone would subscribe |
| the problem exists without the trend | collapses when the trend fades |

Signs a group is about to grow (need several at once): related keywords rising together · several *new* apps gaining downloads ·
revenue rising with downloads · a recurring complaint nobody solves well · a new technology, API, device or behaviour · a frequent
problem (retention) · value easy to show in an ad or screenshot.

## 5. The conclusion
End §2 with:
```
**Kết luận bước 2:** đào tiếp / dừng sớm — <một câu lý do>.
So với ngưỡng dừng: <ngưỡng 1 → chưa chạm / đã chạm (#…)> · <ngưỡng 2 → …>
```
If a threshold is hit, stop and present the choice to the user: dừng / đổi hướng (another keyword, tier or segment) / vẫn đào tiếp —
and record their choice and reason in §2. If data to check a threshold is missing, say so and mark "chưa kiểm được — cần <số>". Thresholds are set before looking at the
numbers, and each has a **vùng xám** (between stop and go) with what to do there — usually: get one more number, or continue with the
risk named in §7.

## 6. Outside reference numbers
Context only, never our numbers — always with source and date:
- Store page → install conversion (AppTweak benchmarks, H1 2024, US): iOS average ~25%, Google Play ~27%; Health & Fitness on Google
  Play ~23%. A category far below this usually means weak store pages or low-intent traffic.
- Ratings: ★ 4,0 is roughly the floor for trust and featuring; large competitors under ★ 4,0 = an opening on quality.
Search volume and exact ranks need paid tools (AppStoreSpy / Sensor Tower); never estimate them from memory.
